## 2. First deliverable: independent methodological audit

Before redesigning the current system, **validate and extend the committed literature survey**, consulting primary papers and original author code. The existing survey already lists many relevant approaches; **do not consume the 30% budget repeating that literature search from scratch**. Maintain a compact evidence inventory of at least **10 relevant approaches**, of which **at least 5 directly address collision/conflict assessment rather than only detection, MOT or optical flow**. Inspect original equations, camera assumptions, source availability, checkpoint and license status, input/output contracts, datasets, and what was actually validated. In a compact evidence table label each candidate:

- **Reusable official code** (with license and exact pinned version);
- **Faithful equation-based reproduction possible**;
- **Incomplete specification / informed adaptation only**;
- **Incompatible with current data**, stating the precise missing measurements.

Do **not** dismiss a method merely because its original paper used a simulator, an auxiliary sensor, or AOT; distinguish reusing its algorithm from reproducing its published evaluation. Conversely, do not present a sensor-assisted or 3-D-state method as a monocular RGB-only solution. **Document why each shortlisted candidate is or is not reproducible.**

Mandatory starting points:

1. **Bauer et al. (2016 and 2019)** — monocular time-to-collision / closest-approach estimation and flight collision decision. Verify original definitions, full geometry, validity conditions and thresholds rather than reconstructing the current estimator under a new name.
2. **AirTrack (Ghosh et al., ICRA 2023)** — its reported angular-state, apparent expansion and `tCPA` cues. **AirTrack is principally an airborne detection/tracking system, not necessarily a complete independent collision classifier.** Reproduce its published threat-related cues faithfully where specified; test whether its `tCPA` means camera-plane contact rather than actual 3-D closest approach. Do not claim official code/weights exist unless verified.
3. **Lai & Lin (2024)** — image-based mid-air risk assessment, conflict regions and avoidance rules; reproduce the published logic if fully specified, explicitly document missing elements.
4. **Opromolla & Fasano (2021)** — visual conflict detection with LOS-rate/ownship navigation; evaluate separately where required synchronized sensors are actually available.
5. **Stanford SISL VisionBasedAircraftDAA**, **ColANet**, **Purdue UAV-to-UAV**, and other genuinely relevant recent (2024–2026) methods. Use Purdue primarily as a moving-camera detection/tracking/ego-motion comparator, not an invented ground-truth collision algorithm.

**Research gate:** Produce a written selection rationale and an executable comparative plan before investing in a new custom estimator, changing risk thresholds, training a new network or polishing the video UI. A literature list alone is not an acceptable completion of this gate.

## 4. Falsification before optimization

All methods must face the existing project's **wide-bearing false-clear** and **camera-parallax** counterexamples. Add analytical tests with independently computed true closest approach (under a clearly specified 3-D constant-relative-velocity model) for: head-on, crossing, overtaking, large off-axis bearing, target recession, lateral fly-by, rotation/parallax, target-size/aspect variation, missing measurements, clipping, detector noise and unequal sample spacing. Sweep assumptions rather than reporting a few selected passes.

A wrong `apparent_nonconflict` where **physical ground truth demonstrates a collision/near miss under the specified model** is a critical false-safe failure, regardless of an attractive average score. State precisely whether a counterexample violates the published algorithm's valid operating assumptions. Restrict unsupported output to `uncertain` where necessary; **do not manufacture positive evidence or quietly change definitions to inflate coverage**.

A monocular image sequence with arbitrary ego translation, unknown 3-D target shape and no independent range **cannot in general uniquely determine physical CPA**. Do not assert otherwise. A well-supported result that a particular distinction is unobservable is valid research; but repeatedly suppressing all `apparent_nonconflict` on real moving-camera video without investigating alternative motion models is **not sufficient engineering progress**.
